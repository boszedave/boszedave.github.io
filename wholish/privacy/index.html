Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dvain Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Wholish on iOS.

1. Information you provide

Wholish does not require you to create an account. The app lets you choose food preferences, dietary preferences, allergens, and watch signals. It also stores your scan history. This information is stored locally on your device and is used to personalize results and show your history.

2. Camera and barcode scanning

Wholish requests camera access to detect product barcodes. Camera frames are processed on your device for barcode recognition. We do not upload or retain camera images.

After a barcode is detected—or entered manually—the barcode is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve available product information. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts may receive technical request information such as your IP address.

3. Product information

Wholish retrieves product names, images, ingredients, nutrition facts, allergens, categories, processing information, and related product data from Open Food Facts. Comparison searches also query Open Food Facts. Product data and images may be cached locally on your device to improve performance and preserve scan history.

4. Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. Wholish uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and premium access. RevenueCat may process an app user identifier, purchase and subscription status, product identifiers, transaction information, and related device or technical information. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

5. Analytics, advertising, and location

Wholish does not include third-party advertising, does not request or collect location data, and does not currently send app-usage analytics to us. Diagnostic messages used during development remain on the development device and are not enabled as a production analytics service.

6. Third-party services

  • Apple: App distribution, payments, subscriptions, and optional App Store rating prompts. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and entitlement management. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.
  • Open Food Facts: Product lookup, images, and comparison data. See the Open Food Facts privacy information.

7. Data retention and deletion

Preferences, cached product information, and scan history remain on your device until you clear the applicable data in Wholish, uninstall the app, or erase the device. You can clear scan history from the app’s Settings screen. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies and legal obligations.

8. Data security

Network requests use HTTPS encryption in transit. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children’s privacy

Wholish is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. International processing

Apple, RevenueCat, and Open Food Facts may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their respective safeguards and policies.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information processed by service providers, including access, correction, deletion, or objection. You can manage camera access in iOS Settings, clear local history in Wholish, manage subscriptions through Apple, and contact us with privacy questions.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as Wholish or applicable requirements change. The current version and its update date will remain available at this URL.
